About Kara

The baker, the story, and the love behind every loaf and layer.

The Story

Baking as a Way of Life

What started as weekend therapy became something bigger — a kitchen full of flour, a house that always smells like butter and cinnamon, and a growing community of people who keep coming back for more.

Kara's Bakery was born from the belief that food made by hand, with care, just tastes different. Better. More real. Whether it's a sourdough loaf with a 24-hour cold ferment or a birthday cake built from scratch to match your vision, every single thing is made with intention.

Based in Texas, Kara bakes for families, celebrations, gatherings, and the quiet Tuesday when you just need something good. No bakery storefronts, no shortcuts — just real baking, real ingredients, and real love in every bite.
